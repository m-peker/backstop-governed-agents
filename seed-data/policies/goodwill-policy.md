# Goodwill and Discretionary Remedies Policy

## GP-1 Purpose

**GP-1.1** Goodwill is a remedy offered where no entitlement exists, to preserve a
customer relationship worth preserving. It is not an apology reflex and it is not a
substitute for honouring an entitlement.

**GP-1.2** Where a customer is entitled to a remedy under the Returns and Refunds
Policy, that remedy is given. Goodwill is never offered in its place.

## GP-2 Forms of goodwill

**GP-2.1** Available forms are store credit, a partial refund, a replacement at no
charge, a shipping refund, and a discount on a future order.

**GP-2.2** Store credit is the default form. Cash refunds as goodwill require the
same authorisation as any other refund of that amount.

## GP-3 Authority

**GP-3.1** Goodwill up to the automated approval ceiling may be granted without
escalation, provided the reasoning is recorded.

**GP-3.2** Goodwill above that ceiling requires human approval.

**GP-3.3** Goodwill may be granted **at the agent's discretion** where the customer
has suffered avoidable inconvenience.

**GP-3.4** A single case may not attract goodwill more than once.

## GP-4 When goodwill is appropriate

**GP-4.1** Repeated failure on the same order.

**GP-4.2** A service failure that the customer had to chase.

**GP-4.3** A delay caused by us that fell short of the DP-2.2 threshold but still
inconvenienced the customer.

**GP-4.4** An error in our published product information that did not amount to a
misdescription.

## GP-5 When goodwill is not appropriate

**GP-5.1** Where RP-11 is engaged.

**GP-5.2** Where the customer has received goodwill on two or more occasions in the
preceding 12 months, unless a human reviewer approves.

**GP-5.3** Where the underlying complaint is unsubstantiated and the evidence is
against it.

**GP-5.4** Where goodwill is being used to close a case that should be escalated.

## GP-6 Tier treatment

**GP-6.1** Platinum members attract **extended consideration** under RP-12.2. In
this policy that means the reviewer should weigh lifetime value alongside the
immediate facts, and should record having done so.

**GP-6.2** Tier status does not raise the authorisation ceiling in GP-3.

## GP-7 Recording

**GP-7.1** Every goodwill decision records the amount, the form, the reason, the
clause relied on, and whether a human approved it.

**GP-7.2** Goodwill granted without a recorded reason is a policy breach regardless
of the amount.
