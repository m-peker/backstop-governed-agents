# Returns and Refunds Policy

## RP-1 Scope

**RP-1.1** This policy governs returns, refunds, replacements and store credit for
all consumer orders placed through our online store and delivered within Türkiye.

**RP-1.2** Where this policy conflicts with a mandatory consumer right, the
mandatory right prevails. Nothing here limits a customer's statutory withdrawal
period.

**RP-1.3** Orders placed by business accounts are governed by the commercial terms
in their supply agreement and are out of scope.

## RP-2 Standard return window

**RP-2.1** A customer may return an eligible item within **30 calendar days of
delivery** for a full refund of the item price.

**RP-2.2** Where delivery cannot be evidenced, the window runs from 5 calendar days
after dispatch.

**RP-2.3** Original outbound shipping is refunded only where the return arises from
our error or a product fault. Return shipping is free in all cases.

**RP-2.4** Gold and Platinum members receive a **60 calendar day** window on all
items other than those excluded under RP-6.

## RP-3 Condition of returned goods

**RP-3.1** Items must be returned unused, in resaleable condition, with all tags,
seals and original packaging intact.

**RP-3.2** Items showing signs of use beyond reasonable inspection may be refused,
or refunded in part with a deduction reflecting the loss in value.

**RP-3.3** Reasonable inspection means the handling a customer could have carried
out in a physical store. Trying a garment on is reasonable inspection. Wearing it
outdoors is not.

## RP-4 Items damaged on arrival

**RP-4.1** Where an item arrives damaged, the customer is entitled to a full refund
or a replacement at their election, including all shipping costs.

**RP-4.2** Damage must be reported **within 48 hours of delivery** so that a claim
can be raised with the carrier while it remains admissible.

**RP-4.3** Photographic evidence should be requested but its absence is not on its
own grounds for refusal.

**RP-4.4** No deduction may be applied to a damaged-on-arrival refund.

## RP-5 Items not received

**RP-5.1** Where a customer reports that an order was not received, the delivery
record must be examined before any resolution is offered.

**RP-5.2** Where the carrier record shows no delivery scan, or shows delivery to an
address other than the one on the order, the claim is upheld and a full refund or
replacement is issued.

**RP-5.3** Where the carrier record shows a completed delivery supported by a
captured signature **or** a delivery photograph, the claim is referred for review
rather than settled automatically.

**RP-5.4** A referral under RP-5.3 is not a refusal. Absent other concerns, a
first-time claim from an account in good standing should be resolved in the
customer's favour.

## RP-6 Excluded items

**RP-6.1** Items marked **final sale** at the point of purchase may not be returned
except where faulty or damaged on arrival.

**RP-6.2** Hygiene-sensitive items, including cosmetics and personal care products,
may not be returned once any seal is broken.

**RP-6.3** Perishable goods, including food and drink, may not be returned unless
they arrived spoiled, damaged or past their date.

**RP-6.4** An exclusion under this section never defeats a claim for an item that
was faulty, damaged on arrival, or not the item ordered.

## RP-7 Wrong item received

**RP-7.1** Where the item delivered is not the item ordered, the customer is
entitled to a replacement of the correct item or a full refund, at their election.

**RP-7.2** Collection of the incorrect item is arranged at our cost. The refund is
not conditional on its return.

## RP-8 Refund method and timing

**RP-8.1** Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

**RP-8.2** Where the original method is unavailable, store credit may be offered
with the customer's agreement.

**RP-8.3** Orders paid cash on delivery are refunded by bank transfer to an account
in the customer's own name.

**RP-8.4** Refunds are initiated within 3 business days of approval. Settlement
times are set by the payment provider and are outside our control.

## RP-9 Partial refunds

**RP-9.1** A partial refund may be issued where an item is returned in diminished
condition under RP-3.2, or where only part of a multi-item order is returned.

**RP-9.2** Any deduction must be stated to the customer with the reason before the
refund is issued.

## RP-10 Replacements

**RP-10.1** A replacement is dispatched once the return is approved. Return of the
original item is not a precondition where the item was faulty, damaged or wrong.

**RP-10.2** Where the correct item is out of stock, a full refund is issued.

## RP-11 Return abuse

**RP-11.1** We may decline a return, or refer an account for review, where the
pattern of a customer's returns indicates abuse rather than genuine dissatisfaction.

**RP-11.2** Indicators include a return rate far above the norm, repeated
high-value returns at the very end of the window, several accounts filing claims
from a single delivery address, and repeated non-receipt claims against deliveries
the carrier can evidence.

**RP-11.3** No single indicator is sufficient. A decision under this section must
rest on more than one indicator and must be recorded with its reasoning.

**RP-11.4** An account may not be declined under this section without human review.
Automated systems may refer, gather evidence and recommend; they may not refuse.

## RP-12 Loyalty tier treatment

**RP-12.1** Gold and Platinum members receive the extended window set out in
RP-2.4.

**RP-12.2** Platinum members are entitled to **extended consideration** on
discretionary decisions.

**RP-12.3** Tier status never overrides RP-11.

## RP-13 Escalation

**RP-13.1** A case must be escalated to a human reviewer where the amount at stake
exceeds the automated approval ceiling, where RP-11 is engaged, where the applicable
clauses conflict, or where the customer disputes an earlier decision.

**RP-13.2** A customer may request human review of any decision at any point, and
the request must be honoured.
