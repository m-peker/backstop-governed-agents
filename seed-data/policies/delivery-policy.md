# Delivery and Carrier Claims Policy

## DP-1 Scope

**DP-1.1** This policy covers dispatch, delivery, delivery evidence and claims
raised against a carrier. It governs the factual determination of what happened to
a parcel; the customer remedy that follows is set by the Returns and Refunds
Policy.

## DP-2 Delivery promise

**DP-2.1** Standard delivery is quoted as 2 to 5 business days from dispatch.
Express delivery is quoted as next business day where ordered before 14:00.

**DP-2.2** A delivery is **late** when it arrives more than 3 business days after
the latest quoted date.

**DP-2.3** The quoted window excludes public holidays and periods where a carrier
has declared a force majeure event.

## DP-3 Delivery evidence

**DP-3.1** A delivery is evidenced by a carrier scan recording the time, the
geolocation of the handset, and at least one of a captured signature or a
photograph of the parcel at the delivery point.

**DP-3.2** A scan alone, without a signature or a photograph, is weak evidence. It
establishes that the carrier believes it delivered the parcel; it does not
establish receipt by the customer.

**DP-3.3** Delivery to a neighbour, a concierge, or a parcel locker is delivery for
carrier purposes but **not** proof of receipt by the customer.

**DP-3.4** Evidence is retained for 90 days from delivery. After that it cannot be
produced and a claim must be assessed without it.

## DP-4 Parcels lost in transit

**DP-4.1** A parcel with no movement scan for 10 consecutive days is treated as
lost, whether or not the customer has raised a claim.

**DP-4.2** A parcel declared lost by the carrier is settled with the customer
immediately. The carrier claim is pursued separately and never delays the customer
remedy.

**DP-4.3** Where a parcel later surfaces and is delivered after settlement, the
customer is invited to return it at our cost or keep it. No charge is raised
without agreement.

## DP-5 Claims of non-receipt

**DP-5.1** A non-receipt claim must be assessed against the evidence in DP-3, not
against the customer's history alone.

**DP-5.2** Where evidence under DP-3.1 is complete, the case is referred for review
under RP-5.3.

**DP-5.3** Where evidence is weak under DP-3.2, or the delivery point was a
neighbour, concierge or locker under DP-3.3, the claim is upheld.

**DP-5.4** The carrier claim window is 14 days from the delivery scan. A claim
raised after that window cannot be recovered from the carrier, but this has no
bearing on the customer remedy.

## DP-6 Late delivery

**DP-6.1** Where a delivery is late under DP-2.2, the shipping charge is refunded
on request.

**DP-6.2** Where a late delivery caused the item to lose its purpose, and the
customer states this at the time of the claim, the order may be refunded in full
and collection arranged at our cost.

**DP-6.3** Lateness caused by a declared force majeure event does not attract
compensation, but the customer retains every other right under this policy and the
Returns and Refunds Policy.

## DP-7 Address errors

**DP-7.1** Where the customer supplied an incorrect address, redelivery is charged
at the standard rate.

**DP-7.2** Where our systems or the carrier transcribed the address incorrectly,
redelivery is free and the customer may cancel for a full refund.
